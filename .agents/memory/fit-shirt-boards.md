---
name: FIT-HPUni shirt boards conventions
description: Rules for the faculty shirt design boards on the canvas (garment flats, logo use, constraints)
---
- Garment drawings must come from the shared mm-accurate flats so all concepts share silhouettes and scale.
  **Why:** concepts are compared side by side by the faculty; differing silhouettes would bias the choice and break real-cm callouts.
  **How to apply:** new garments/variants import PoloShirt/TeeShirt; extend the shared file only additively (other boards depend on it).
- Logo is used unaltered (no redraw/recolor); only an embroidered-look filter is allowed.
  **Why:** official faculty mark; the user asked for designs around the real logo.
- Production realism is a hard requirement (embroidery ≥5 mm text, ≤4 screen colours, back print ≤A3, prints don't cross side seams).
  **Why:** user asked for expert-level, manufacturable designs.
- Internal concept names (Dấu Ấn, do IT, Phượng Số) are board labels only — never printed on the garment; garment text identifies the faculty (FIT-HPUni / faculty name) and the verbatim slogan.
  **Why:** a design subagent printed "PHƯỢNG SỐ" on a tee back; the shirt must represent the faculty, not our pitch vocabulary.
  **How to apply:** review garment copy on every new board/variant before presenting.
- On 2026-10-16 the user asked for "phiên bản mà sinh viên yêu thích nhất" → student edition "Hoa Học Trò" (C's phượng + B's "do IT" + personalised name). Treat student appeal as a stated priority for follow-up work.
  **Why:** the user's own request after seeing A/B/C.
- For the Đồng Điệu direction, omit city symbols and focus on the faculty slogan: “You can do IT, we are ready to accompany”.
  **Why:** the user explicitly redirected this selected design away from city imagery.
  **How to apply:** keep future refinements of this direction slogan-led; this does not require changing the other concept boards.
- Với cổ polo Đồng Điệu, vẽ cổ rộng hơn để chứa tem, không thu nhỏ tem để tránh chồng lấn.
  **Why:** người dùng sửa lại hướng xử lý: “tôi muốn vẽ lại cổ cho rộng ra thay vì chỉnh nhỏ tem”.
  **How to apply:** giữ tem dễ đọc và điều chỉnh hình học cổ khi xử lý khoảng trống quanh tem.
